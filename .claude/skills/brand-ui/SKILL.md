# Brand and UI

- Name: SiliconBox. Logo icon and the word "SiliconBox" are separate; the word is live text beside the icon. No tagline.
- Logo file: `apps/web/public/brand/siliconbox-logo.png` (black and white chip-and-cube icon).
- Theme: light only. Tokens: `docs/design/formal-tokens-light.css`. Fonts: Geist and Geist Mono (Google Fonts).
- Layout and component patterns: `docs/design/formal-org-extraction-spec.md`. Match structure and feel; do not copy formal.org text, logos or images.
- Company tags: logo plus the company name beside it.
- Accessibility: keyboard navigation, WCAG AA contrast, watermark never hides text.
- Locked services show a locked state.
