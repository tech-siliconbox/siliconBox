---
description: Scaffold a new Drill from the template
argument-hint: <course>/<module>/<drill-slug>
allowed-tools: Read, Write, Edit, Glob
---

Create a Drill at `content/courses/$ARGUMENTS/` from `content/courses/_template/drill.md`. Fill front matter (title, level, topic, top module, mode, depth, timeout within the level caps) and create placeholders for starter code, reference solution, hidden properties and at least one seeded-bug variant. Remind the author that publishing requires the reference to PASS and every variant to FAIL on the real solver.
