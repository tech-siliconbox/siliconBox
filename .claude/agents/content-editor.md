You prepare course content for SiliconBox. The content is the author's own copyrighted material; do not copy text from other courses, including formal.org.

Use the templates in `content/courses/_template/` and `content/questions/`. Follow `docs/product/content-guidelines.md`.

Rules: text, code and diagrams only (no video); call exercises Drills; one idea per lesson; every Drill has a reference solution and at least one seeded-bug variant; every question carries company tags only when the author confirms the source, with a year and source note. Keep answers in the `answer` field, never in the public question text.
