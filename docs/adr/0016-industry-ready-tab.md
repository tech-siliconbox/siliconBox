# 0016. Industry Ready as its own tab; free tools for buyers only

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-01

## Context

Career services are not all designed yet.

## Decision

Separate tab with cards. Resume Builder and CV screening are free only for learners with an active content entitlement; other services are locked with an admin-controlled status.

## Consequences

Locked services must refuse every call. Free tools hold CVs, so they need per-user storage, deletion and rate limits.
