# Solver down

Symptoms: runs stay queued, time out or error for many learners.

1. Check the status page, queue depth and runner health.
2. Show a banner and keep lessons readable; Drills pause.
3. Restart runners or scale them. On Render free, expect about a minute to wake.
4. Check for abuse: one account or IP creating many runs. Lock it and review.
5. After recovery, confirm queued jobs completed or were cleanly failed. Offer learners an extension of the tool window if the outage was long (admin grant with a reason).
6. Write a short postmortem.
