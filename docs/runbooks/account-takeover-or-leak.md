# Account takeover or content leak

## Takeover

1. Revoke all sessions for the account.
2. Force password reset and offer TOTP.
3. Check the audit log for purchases, downloads and changes.
4. Notify the learner by email.

## Leak of paid content

1. Find the copy, read the visible mark, and look up the invisible pattern in the private trace table.
2. Suspend the traced account pending review; keep evidence.
3. Decide action under the terms of use (warning, termination).
4. Rotate anything exposed. Look for pacing or anomaly alerts that should have fired and tune them.
5. If personal data was exposed, follow legal notification duties (DPDP Act) and notify affected people.
6. Write a short postmortem.
