# Refund policy checklist

- [ ] Decide the refund window and conditions (for example, before a set amount of content is used)
- [ ] Say what a refund does: entitlements for the refunded purchase are revoked through the refund webhook
- [ ] Upgrade refunds: difference only, and what remains active
- [ ] How to ask and how long it takes
- [ ] Payment-gateway fees and who bears them
- [ ] Publish on the pricing page and link from the checkout
