# Terms of use checklist

- [ ] Account: one person per account; one active device; no sharing of credentials
- [ ] Content: licence to learners for personal use only; copyright stays with the author
- [ ] Forbidden: copying, recording, redistributing, scraping, automated access, reselling
- [ ] Consequence: traced leaks can lead to suspension or termination without refund
- [ ] Watermarking disclosed
- [ ] Access windows and what happens when they end
- [ ] Fair use of the formal tool: quotas, timeouts, no abuse
- [ ] Liability limits and governing law
- [ ] Changes to terms and notice
